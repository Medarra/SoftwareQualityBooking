List of Prompts Used for Application:
1. I'm looking to create a booking application with a front end and a back end. These are the requirements:

This will be a web application that customers and clients will use to book appointments with
service providers (for example a hair salon or barbershop).
The requirements obtained thus far by the Product Owner are:
 Patrons can register or book anonymously (the requirement to register can be set by the
vendor)
 Patrons and service providers can Create, Change, and Cancel appointments
 Appointments can be selected from a variety of service providers (for example choosing
your preferred stylist)
 Show service providers’ availability (once the appointment has been booked, that
availability block is no longer available and is freed if the appointment is canceled)
 Service providers set the availability block duration
 Patrons can set appointments for other individuals with other service providers
concurrently (provided availability).
